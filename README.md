One day's world news in one drawn image: Monday 23 November 2026.

`main.ts` draws the front page itself — a pixel buffer, a 5x7 bitmap
alphabet, and a hand-rolled PNG writer, all standard-library Node, no
network and no assets — so two runs write byte-identical output. The
six stories: the COP31 talks collapsing in Antalya, NATO's emergency
meeting after Russian missiles entered Polish airspace, the rising
death toll in Dymer, tankers hitting mines near the Strait of Hormuz,
a second drone strike off Benghazi, and South Korea's education
minister resigning over an AI exam-cheating scandal, with markets, the
UN killer-robot talks, the Indonesia factory fire, the Nepal–India
talks, and the Liverpool–Manchester United result in the foot strip.
